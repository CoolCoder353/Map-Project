Wayfinder is a map for people who like to explore. It remembers the roads you've travelled and points you to the ones you haven't.

• Turn-by-turn directions on your phone and in your car through Android Auto
• Explore routes: a slightly longer way that takes in new roads
• Discover nearby places in areas you haven't been
• See the roads you've covered and replay past trips
• Spoken directions with lane-level turn icons and roundabout exits

Your trips stay on the group's own server. Routing, maps and search all run there, with no ads and no outside tracking. Recording trips in the background is off until you turn it on.

Wayfinder is invite-only: you need an invite code from the group running your server. Maps currently cover Queensland, Australia.
